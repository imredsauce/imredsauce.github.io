THE GAME ROOM

Pick your next round.

Start with a slash command. Play with buttons, watch the reveal, and see the result right in Discord.

🎲

Dice Duel

Two members stake the same XP. High roll wins. Ties refund both.

/dice
🎰

Slots

Three reels. Match all three symbols to win the configured payout.

/slot
🎟️

Scratch Card

Click to reveal each of the three spots. Matching all three pays 2×.

/scratch
🎡

Spin Wheel

Spin for a multiplier or a free re-spin. Miss loses the stake.

/wheel
💣

Minesweeper

Pick safe tiles on a 5×4 board. Cash out before you hit a mine.

/mines
🃏

High–Low

Guess the next card’s rank. Ties return your stake.

/highlow
♠️

Blackjack

Hit, stand, double down, or split a pair. Natural blackjack pays 2.5×.

/blackjack
🗼

Tower

Pick a door on each floor. Climb safely and cash out before finding a trap.

/tower
🏇

Horse Race

Open a shared race. Members get 10 seconds to back a horse after the host picks.

/horse
💣

Bomb Defusal

Guess the three unique digits. Each attempt reveals exact and misplaced matches.

/defuse
🎯

Russian Roulette

Two to six players take turns. Last player standing wins the XP pot.

/roulette
🂠

Indian Poker

See their card, hide yours. Each player can raise once and must have 3× the opening stake.

/poker
❌

Tic-Tac-Toe

Two players take turns on a 3×3 board. Three in a row wins. Draws refund both stakes.

/tictactoe
